Words I should have said

A letter, from my heart

Dear Shahnaz,

Mujhe tumse dil se maafi maangni hai. Jab maine tumse kaha tha ki meri shaadi kahin aur ho rahi hai, tab maine tumhe bahut takleef pahunchayi. Main samajh sakta hoon ki meri woh baat tumhare liye kitni dard bhari aur uljhan wali rahi hogi, khaaskar jab humare beech ek apna sa rishta tha.

Main apni galti ke liye koi bahana nahi banana chahta. Sirf sorry keh dene se jo kuch hua, woh theek nahi ho sakta. Mujhe tumse zyada sachchai aur tumhari feelings ka zyada khayal rakhna chahiye tha. Tum is tarah hurt hone ki bilkul haqdaar nahi thi.

Hum abhi bhi baat karte hain, lekin main samajhta hoon ki baat karne ka matlab yeh nahi ki sab kuch pehle jaisa ho gaya hai. Main yeh dikhawa nahi karna chahta ki kuch hua hi nahi, aur na hi tumse yeh umeed karta hoon ki tum bas sab bhula do.

Mujhe sach mein afsos hai ki meri wajah se tumhe takleef hui, Shahnaz. Tum jo bhi mehsoos karti ho ya jo bhi faisla leti ho, main uski izzat karunga. Tum par mujhe maaf karne, jawab dene ya mujhe ek aur mauka dene ka koi pressure nahi hai.

With sincerity,
Someone who should have handled your heart with more care.

“Main beete hue kal ko badal nahi sakta, lekin apni galti ki zimmedari le sakta hoon aur aage se zyada sachcha reh sakta hoon.”
